ABOUT BLOSS@M

BloSS@M focuses on a coordinated approach for managing software assets across U.S. Government agencies. Rather than relying on independent, agency-specific procurement and license management processes. The BloSS@M model explores a software leasing service in which blockchain technology is used to record software asset lifecycle information and to support the controlled distribution of software usage rights to authorized users.

Challenges Addressed

The BloSS@M model tackles long-standing challenges in government software management:

🔧 Fragmented Processes

Eliminates fragmented agency-specific procurement and license management

👁️ Limited Visibility

Provides shared visibility into software acquisition and usage across agencies

🔒 Trust Limitations

Uses blockchain technology to support coordination where participating departments, agencies, vendors, and publishers may not fully trust one another

By enabling shared visibility and coordinated management, BloSS@M seeks to support:

📊 Improved Accountability

Better tracking and responsibility for software assets

📝 Better Negotiations

More informed contract discussions with vendors

🛡️ Enhanced Risk Management

Stronger software supply chain security oversight





BloSS@M's permissioned blockchain requires two distinct private channels, each governed by its own set of smart contracts which implements NGAC:

  • Authorization Channel: Dedicated to the assessment and authorization (A&A) process, where peer nodes prove and continuously maintain their security posture in line with the Federal Information Security Modernization Act (FISMA).

    The authorization channel supports automated security assessments, member authorization, and continuous Authority to Operate (ATO) requirements.

  • Assets Channel: Focused on the core business functions of BloSS@M, supporting the implementation of its operational and technical capabilities.

    The asset channel supports BloSS@M services and its core capabilities, allowing users to discover, compare and securely lease licenses for software assets, and to manage such assets during their use time.



BloSS@M Capabilities

BloSS@M brings a tamper-resistant, blockchain-backed approach to software asset management across U.S. Government agencies — unifying fragmented procurement, licensing, and security data into a single auditable framework.

What BloSS@M Delivers

Immutable Asset Tracking

Every software asset event (acquisition, deployment, update, retirement) is written to a distributed ledger. No single agency or vendor owns the record. Any authorized party can verify and none can alter.

Continuous Threat Correlation

BloSS@M queries the NVD in real time and maps findings against MITRE ATT&CK techniques and MITRE D3FEND countermeasures. This gives agencies an up-to-date picture of their exposure without manual research overhead.

AI-Assisted Risk Assessment

Agentic and generative AI runs provisional security assessments against implemented controls, surfacing control gaps and adversarial alignment issues at a speed and scale no manual review process can match.

OSCAL-Native Security Data

Security control implementations are ingested as OSCAL Component Definition artifacts, which are machine-readable, interoperable, and directly comparable across vendors and agencies. No inconsistent formats or manual translations required.

Cross-Agency Software Comparison

Cost, licensing terms, and security posture data are unified into one framework, enabling objective, evidence-based comparisons of software tools serving similar functions across the federal enterprise.

Shared Software Leasing Model

Software licenses are treated as managed government resources. They are provisioned centrally and reassigned as mission needs shift. Agencies get what they need and unused entitlements are left untouched.


BloSS@M aligns with OMB Circular A-130, NIST SP 800-37 and 800-53, and recent Executive Order mandates on software supply chain security, satisfying compliance requirements while delivering operational value.
