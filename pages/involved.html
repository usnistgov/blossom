GET INVOLVED

To get started with adapting blossom to your needs, go to the blossom repository on GitHub.​

This repository contains submodules for the different aspects of blossom. Depending on your specific needs, you may use any, all, or none of these repositories. You may also fork the repositories to customize to your needs. See below for descriptions of the submodules.

  • blossom-member - to join the chain
  • blossom-oscal - for OSCAL content that supports blossom, such as user management automation, assessment
  • blossom-dashboard - a dashboard to query and view users on the chain
  • blossom-chaincode and blossom-sam - to deploy your own chain, rather than joining
