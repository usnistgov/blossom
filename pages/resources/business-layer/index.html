BUSINESS LAYER

BloSS@M Business Layer

The BloSS@M business layer represents the collection of operational and technical capabilities that enable coordinated software asset management, supply chain risk visibility, and automation-enabled cybersecurity governance.

These capabilities work together to provide organizations with greater transparency, accountability, and assurance across the software asset lifecycle while supporting enterprise-wide decision-making.

Key Purpose: Establishing a trusted environment for software asset governance that supports the entire software asset lifecycle while enabling enterprise-wide decision-making through enhanced transparency and automation.

Supporting Enterprise Software Asset Governance

The business layer enables organizations to manage software assets through policy-governed processes that promote consistency, accountability, and operational efficiency.

By providing authoritative visibility into software assets and associated licensing information, BloSS@M helps organizations make informed decisions regarding software procurement, deployment, utilization, and retirement.

These capabilities support the governance of both Information Technology (IT) and Operational Technology (OT) environments, where maintaining accurate software inventories and ensuring compliance with organizational policies are critical to security and operational effectiveness.

As software ecosystems continue to expand across organizational, contractual, and geographic boundaries, organizations require reliable mechanisms to manage software assets, monitor software supply chain risks, and maintain visibility into software usage and licensing.

Enhancing Transparency and Risk Visibility

🔍 A key objective of the BloSS@M business layer is to improve transparency across software supply chains.

BloSS@M provides mechanisms for tracking software assets and software license entitlements across departments, agencies, vendors, publishers, and other stakeholders that may operate in low-trust or trustless environments.

This visibility enables organizations to better understand the origin, ownership, distribution, and usage of software assets while improving awareness of software supply chain risks.

Secure License Leasing and Asset Distribution

BloSS@M supports secure and cost-effective software license leasing through controlled distribution and lifecycle management processes.

BloSS@M enables the downstream allocation and management of leased software licenses while maintaining governance controls designed to preserve the integrity, availability, and confidentiality of software asset information.

Benefit: Organizations can optimize software utilization and improve license accountability through controlled distribution mechanisms.

Policy-Governed Trust and Accountability

The business layer incorporates blockchain-enabled capabilities tailored to federal software asset management and security requirements.

Unlike conventional blockchain approaches that may expose sensitive operational information, BloSS@M combines tamper-evident records with policy-based access controls to ensure that software asset information remains protected while still supporting transparency and auditability.

This approach enables organizations to maintain authoritative records of software assets and transactions while enforcing least-privilege access principles and protecting sensitive information from unauthorized disclosure.

Security Feature: Tamper-evident records with policy-based access controls protect sensitive information while maintaining transparency.

Automation and Continuous Assurance

⚙️ BloSS@M strengthens trust through automation and machine-readable software asset evidence.

By integrating standardized software identification mechanisms, including ISO/IEC 19770-2 Software Identification (SWID) tags, the platform supports the continuous collection of information related to software installation, usage, updates, and removal.

These automated capabilities improve auditability and accountability by providing verifiable evidence of software asset activity throughout the lifecycle.

🔍 Organizations can use this information to identify unauthorized changes, detect potential license misuse, and monitor deviations from approved software configurations.

Automation Benefit: Continuous collection of software asset evidence improves auditability and accountability throughout the asset lifecycle.

Business Outcomes

Collectively, the capabilities within the BloSS@M business layer help organizations:

  • Improve software asset visibility across enterprise environments.
  • Strengthen software supply chain risk awareness and governance.
  • Support secure and accountable software license leasing.
  • Enhance auditability through authoritative and tamper-evident records.
  • Enable policy-based access to sensitive software asset information.
  • Increase confidence in software inventory and licensing data.
  • Automate evidence collection for compliance and governance activities.
  • Support enterprise-wide decision-making through trusted software asset intelligence.
